# Exercise Recipe Reference

Standard defaults for common exercises. Use these as baseline values when generating cassettes.
The health project can override any value — these exist so the format stays consistent when the
project doesn't specify.

If an exercise isn't listed, use Claude's exercise knowledge and note the assumption. For rest
times, use the closest comparable movement's value.

---

## Warmup Exercises

General-purpose warmup movements. A good warmup prepares the joints and movement patterns used
in the main work. Typical warmup: 1-2 rounds as a circuit, 30s rest between rounds, 2-3
exercises selected to match the main work.

| Exercise | Rest (s) | Form Cues |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| Band Pull-Aparts | 30 | Squeeze scapulae together, arms straight, controlled tempo. |
| Band Dislocates | 30 | Wide grip, slow arc overhead and behind. Only go as far as mobility allows. |
| Light Goblet Squat | 30 | 50% of working weight or less. Focus on depth and hip opening, elbows push knees out. |
| Bodyweight Squat | 30 | Full depth, controlled. Ease into the range of motion. |
| Dead Hang | 45 | Relaxed shoulders, decompress the spine. Breathe. |
| Cat-Cow | 30 | Slow, articulate each vertebra. Match movement to breath. |
| World's Greatest Stretch | 30 | Lunge, rotate, reach. Hits hips, thoracic spine, and hamstrings. |
| Hip 90/90 | 30 | Sit tall, rotate between internal and external rotation. Don't force the range. |
| Arm Circles | 15 | Small to large, forward and back. Gets synovial fluid moving. |
| Glute Bridges | 30 | Squeeze at top, hold 1-2s. Activates posterior chain before heavy work. |

### Warmup Selection Guide

Match warmup exercises to the main work:

| Main Work Focus | Warmup Priorities |
|----------------|-------------------|
| Upper push (bench, OHP) | Band pull-aparts, band dislocates, arm circles |
| Upper pull (rows, pull-ups) | Band pull-aparts, dead hang, arm circles |
| Lower (squat, deadlift, RDL) | Bodyweight squat, hip 90/90, glute bridges |
| Full body | Band pull-aparts, bodyweight squat, dead hang |
| Isometric/core heavy | Cat-cow, glute bridges, dead hang |

## Compound Movements — Barbell / Dumbbell

| Exercise | Rest (s) | Form Cues |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| Barbell Squat | 150 | Brace before descent, break at hips and knees simultaneously, depth to parallel or below. |
| Goblet Squat | 105 | Elbows between knees at bottom, chest up, full depth. |
| DB Bench Press | 105 | Scapulae retracted, slight arch, feet flat. Control the eccentric. |
| Barbell Bench Press | 150 | Same cues as DB. Unrack with locked arms, touch chest, press to lockout. |
| Seated DB OHP | 105 | Lockout overhead, head through at top. Don't lean back excessively. |
| Barbell OHP | 105 | Brace core, lockout overhead, head through. |
| One-Arm DB Row | 75 | Pull to hip, full stretch at bottom, squeeze at top. Don't rotate the torso. |
| Barbell Row | 105 | Hip hinge, pull to lower chest, squeeze scapulae. |
| Incline DB Press | 105 | 30-45° bench. Scapulae set, press up and slightly back, full lockout. Control the eccentric. |
| DB Floor Press | 90 | Shoulder-safe pressing. Upper arm touches floor each rep, brief pause, press. Limited ROM by design. |
| Chest-Supported DB Row | 75 | Chest on incline bench. Pure pull — no torso momentum available. Squeeze at top. |
| Arnold Press | 105 | Rotate palms from facing you to facing forward through the press. Slightly lighter than standard OHP. |
| DB RDL | 105 | Slow eccentric, hinge at hips not knees, flat back, feel the hamstring stretch. |
| DB Stiff-Leg Deadlift | 105 | Minimal knee bend, deeper hamstring stretch than RDL. Reduce load vs RDL. |
| DB Walking Lunges | 90 | Per-leg. Long stride, torso upright, knee tracks over toes. |
| DB Step-Ups | 90 | Per-leg. Full foot on box, drive through the heel, control the descent — don't drop. |
| Barbell Deadlift | 150 | Brace before pull, push the floor away, lockout with glutes. |
| Hip Thrust | 105 | Full lockout squeeze at top, chin tucked, don't hyperextend the lower back. |
| Bulgarian Split Squat | 105 | Per-leg. Front shin vertical, control the descent, drive through the front heel. |

## Isolation Movements

| Exercise | Rest (s) | Form Cues |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| DB Curls | 60 | Strict form, no swinging, controlled eccentric. Elbows stay at sides. |
| Hammer Curls | 60 | Neutral grip, same strict cues. |
| Tricep Pushdown | 60 | Elbows pinned to sides, full extension, squeeze at bottom. |
| Overhead Tricep Extension | 75 | Elbows pointing forward, lower behind head, full stretch at bottom. |
| Lateral Raises | 45 | Light load, no momentum, slight forward lean, lead with elbows. |
| Face Pulls | 45 | External rotation at peak, pull to forehead level, squeeze rear delts. |
| Leg Curls | 75 | Controlled eccentric, squeeze at peak contraction. |
| Leg Extensions | 75 | Full lockout, controlled tempo. |
| Calf Raises (DB) | 45 | Full ROM, pause at bottom stretch, squeeze at top. |
| Single-Leg Calf Raise | 45 | Per-side. Full ROM, 2s pause at the bottom stretch. |
| Incline DB Curl | 60 | Seated on incline bench, arms hang back — long-length bicep stretch. Strict. |
| DB Pullover | 75 | Slight elbow bend, deep stretch behind head, pull over with lats. Pull-up substitute. |

## Timed / Isometric

| Exercise | Rest (s) | Cue Spacing (s) | Form Cues |
|----------|----------|-----------------|-----------|
| RKC Plank | 90 | 10 | Max tension. Squeeze glutes, engage lats, pull elbows toward toes. |
| Standard Plank | 75 | 15 | Neutral spine, hips level, breathe normally. |
| Dead Hang | 60 | 10 | Relaxed shoulders, deep breaths. Grip work + decompression. |
| Wall Sit | 75 | 10 | 90° knee angle, back flat on wall, don't rest hands on thighs. |
| L-Sit | 75 | 8 | Demanding. Depress shoulders, lock arms. Shorter hold durations typical. |
| Farmer's Carry | 75 | 10 | Tall posture, tight grip, steady breathing, don't let the weight pull you sideways. |
| Copenhagen Plank | 75 | 10 | Adductor focus. Top leg on bench, bottom leg free. Keep hips level. |

## Bodyweight / Calisthenics

| Exercise | Rest (s) | Form Cues |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| Pull-Ups | 105 | Full dead hang at bottom, chin over bar at top. Control the negative. |
| Band-Assisted Pull-Ups | 90 | Band under foot or knee. Same full ROM standards as unassisted. |
| Chin-Ups | 105 | Supinated grip, full ROM. Slightly more bicep emphasis than pull-ups. |
| Dips | 105 | Controlled descent to at least 90° elbow bend, full lockout at top. |
| Push-Ups | 45 | Chest to floor, full lockout, elbows 45° from body. |
| Inverted Rows | 60 | Pull chest to bar, fully extend at bottom. Adjustable difficulty by foot position. |
| Pike Push-Ups | 60 | Hips high, head between arms, vertical pressing pattern. OHP alternative. |

prescribes both exercises in the same session.

| Leg Extensions + Leg Curls | 75 |
